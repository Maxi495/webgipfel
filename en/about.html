About webgipfel

Websites with vision.

Our goal

What we work for

Since founding the agency in 2024, we have pursued one simple goal: building websites that stand out from the crowd. Not by being loud, but through clarity, speed and a design that truly fits your brand. Your online presence belongs at the top – on Google and in your customers’ minds.

Offer less. Deliver better.

What sets us apart

We are not a full-service agency with twenty departments – and we don’t want to be. We do three things: web design, landing pages and SEO. We are genuinely good at them because we focus on nothing else.

With us, you speak directly with the people who design and build your website. No project managers in between, nothing lost in translation. We write our own code and use AI and professional workflows where they make sense, so we reach the goal faster – without cutting corners on quality.

Why webgipfel?

The name

On clear days, you can see the Alps from Munich. That image is at the heart of our name (“Gipfel” is German for “summit”): we want to take websites to the summit – above the many others that are simply online. To us, every project is a climb we make together with you. From the first step all the way to the top.

What you can expect from us

Direct line

You always speak with both of us – the people who actually build your website. Short paths, quick answers, clear agreements.

Our own code

No off-the-shelf website-builder templates. We write the code ourselves and use AI where it saves time – not where it costs quality.

Focus, not a bit of everything

Web design, landing pages and SEO. We deliberately offer nothing more – because we want to be better at these three things than anyone else.

For every industry

Whether a trade business, bakery, restaurant, start-up or mid-sized company: a good website follows the same rules everywhere – and we have mastered them.

Munich & beyond

We are based in Munich and work mainly for clients in Germany – but we are just as happy to work internationally.

Get to know us

Our story

webgipfel is Maximilian Pfeiffer and Aleksandar Djukic – two recent school graduates from Munich who were writing code long before founding the agency. Maximilian has been coding for six years: first apps, then websites, and for the past three years he has been designing them too. Aleksandar has been coding for four years and has already built several landing pages and websites.

In 2024, we turned our shared interest in web design into an agency. We saw how many websites are outdated, slow or interchangeable – and knew we could do better. Today, we support small businesses, freelancers, start-ups and mid-sized companies on their way to the top.
