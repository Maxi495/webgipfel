Last updated: October 18, 2026

We have not appointed a data protection officer. Under § 38(1) BDSG (German Federal Data Protection Act), this obligation only applies if, as a rule, at least twenty persons are constantly engaged in the automated processing of personal data. We do not reach that number — webgipfel is two people.

2. When you simply view this site

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). When you open a page, your browser necessarily transmits data to their servers for technical reasons, where it is recorded in log files:

  • Your IP address
  • Date and time of access
  • The requested address and the response status
  • The amount of data transferred
  • Browser and operating system identifier (user agent)
  • The previously visited page, if your browser sends it (referrer)

We need this data so that the site can be delivered at all and so that we can detect malfunctions and attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the technical operation of the site, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We do not combine these logs with other data and do not create profiles from them.

The logs are deleted as soon as they are no longer needed for secure operation. We do not analyze them and, as a rule, do not access them at all.

3. Fonts

The font used on this site is stored on our own server and delivered from there along with the page. Your browser does not connect to Google or any other font provider for this, and no data is transmitted to third parties in the process.

4. Contact form

If you use the form on our contact page to write to us, we process the information you provide there:

Name and email address
Required, so that we know who is writing to us and can reply.
Message
Required, so that we understand your request.
Company
Optional.
Topic of the enquiry
Optional, if you tick one of the boxes.

The information is sent to us solely as an email and is not used for any other purpose. There is no database in which enquiries would be stored. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, as the processing serves to initiate a contract, as well as your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you confirm when submitting the form. You may withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

To send it, we use the Resend service of Resend, Inc. (USA). The enquiry passes through their servers. It is received and read in an email inbox at Google (Gmail), provided by Google Ireland Limited, Ireland, and Google LLC, USA, respectively.

We delete your enquiry as soon as it has been dealt with, provided no statutory retention obligation prevents this. If your enquiry results in an engagement, the correspondence is subject to the retention periods under German commercial and tax law of six and ten years, respectively (§ 257 HGB – German Commercial Code, § 147 AO – German Fiscal Code).

5. No cookies, no visitor tracking

This site stores nothing on your device: no cookies, no local storage, no session data. No analytics tool, advertising network, social media button or third-party map is embedded. That is why no cookie banner appears when you visit — there is nothing you would need to consent to.

You can check this yourself: in your browser's developer tools, the sections Cookies, Local Storage and Session Storage remain empty for this site.

6. Transfers to the USA

The service providers named in sections 2 to 4 — Vercel, Resend and Google — are US companies or also process data in the USA. This constitutes a transfer to a country outside the European Union. It is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses under Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR.

7. Your rights

You have the right, at any time, to:

  • request information about what data we process about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • have incorrect data rectified (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • have your data erased (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • have the processing restricted (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object to the processing insofar as we base it on a legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR)

An email to webgipfel51@gmail.com is all it takes. You do not need to give reasons for your request, and there is no cost to you.

8. Complaint to a supervisory authority

If you believe that we are not handling your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority is that of the German federal state in which we are based.

You can contact the supervisory authority at your place of residence or work, or the one responsible for our place of business. A list of all German supervisory authorities is maintained by the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

9. Changes

If the technology of this site or the legal situation changes, we will update this policy. The version dated as stated above applies in each case.
