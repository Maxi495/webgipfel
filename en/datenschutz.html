What data is collected when you visit our website, what we use it for, and what rights you have.

As of: October 22, 2026

§ 1 Information on the collection of personal data

(1) Below we inform you about which personal data we collect when you use our website www.webgipfel.com. Personal data is any data that can be related to you personally, for example your name, email address or usage behavior.

(2) The controller within the meaning of Art. 4(7) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

webgipfel

Email: webgipfel51@gmail.com
Website: www.webgipfel.com

We have not appointed a data protection officer because the legal requirements for doing so (§ 38(1) BDSG (German Federal Data Protection Act)) do not apply to us.

(3) If you use email or the form on our contact page to write to us, we process the information you provide: name, email address and message (required) and, optionally, your company and the subject of your inquiry. We use this data solely to respond to your inquiry and, where applicable, to prepare a quote. There is no database in which inquiries are stored; the information is sent to us as an email.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR insofar as your inquiry is aimed at entering into a contract with us; otherwise, our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and the consent you give when submitting the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

We delete your inquiry once it has been dealt with. If no contract is concluded, this happens after six months at the latest. If a contract is concluded, we retain business correspondence for as long as required under commercial and tax law (six or ten years, respectively, pursuant to § 257 HGB, § 147 AO (German Commercial Code, Fiscal Code)).

To send the form, we use the service Resend (Resend, Inc., 2261 Market Street #5039, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA). Inquiries are received and read in an email mailbox hosted by Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland).

(4) Where we use service providers for individual functions of our website, we inform you about this in detail below, in each case including the storage period.

§ 2 Your rights

(1) You have the following rights vis-à-vis us with regard to the personal data concerning you:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) or erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • Right to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR),
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

An informal email to webgipfel51@gmail.com is sufficient.

(2) You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority about our processing of your personal data. The authority responsible for us is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, www.lda.bayern.de. You may also contact the supervisory authority at your place of residence.

§ 3 Collection of personal data when you visit our website

(1) If you merely view our website, i.e. you do not register or otherwise send us information, we only collect the data your browser transmits to the server. This data is technically necessary to display the website to you and to ensure stability and security (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):

  • IP address
  • Date and time of the request
  • Content of the request (page accessed)
  • Access status/HTTP status code
  • Amount of data transferred in each case
  • Website from which the request originates (referrer)
  • Browser, operating system, language and version of the browser software

(2) Our website is hosted by Vercel (Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA). Vercel processes this data on our behalf; we have a data processing agreement with Vercel. The logs are not combined with other data and are automatically deleted after a short time.

(3) The font used on this website is hosted on our own server. Your browser does not connect to Google Fonts or any other font provider for this purpose.

§ 4 Recipients and transfers to third countries

(1) We use external service providers for some of the processing of your data. They have been carefully selected, are bound by our instructions and are regularly monitored. The following categories of recipients may have access to your personal data:

  • Service providers for operating our website and processing the data generated in the process (hosting, email delivery, email mailbox, web analytics – see § 1, § 3 and § 7). The legal basis is Art. 28 GDPR in conjunction with the legal basis for the respective processing stated there.
  • Public bodies and authorities, insofar as this is necessary to fulfill a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Persons we engage for our business operations, for example tax advisors, banks or legal advisors (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR).

(2) Beyond this, we only pass on your personal data to third parties if you have expressly consented pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

(3) Vercel, Resend and Google also process data in the USA. The transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR), insofar as the provider is certified under it, and otherwise on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

§ 5 Objection to or withdrawal of consent for the processing of your data

(1) If you have given consent to the processing of your data, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal takes effect for the future; the lawfulness of processing carried out up to that point remains unaffected. The easiest way to withdraw your consent to Google Analytics is via the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page; otherwise, an email to us is sufficient.

(2) Where we base the processing of your personal data on a balancing of interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object to the processing. When doing so, please tell us the reasons arising from your particular situation. We will then review the matter and either stop or adjust the processing, or show you our compelling legitimate grounds for continuing the processing.

§ 6 Cookies and consent management

(1) On your first visit, we ask you in a notice window whether you want to allow Google Analytics. No cookies are set and no data is transferred to Google before you give your consent. “Reject all” and “Accept all” are displayed side by side with equal prominence; under “Settings” you can decide individually.

(2) We store your decision in your browser's local storage (entry “wg-consent”) for twelve months so that you are not asked again every time you load a page. This storage is strictly necessary to provide the state you requested (§ 25(2)(2) TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act)); the legal basis for the processing is Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 7(1) GDPR (proof of consent). No data is transferred to us or to third parties in the process.

(3) You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

§ 7 Web analytics with Google Analytics 4

(1) Only if you have consented do we use Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). It helps us understand how our website is used so that we can improve it.

(2) Google Analytics uses cookies (_ga and _ga_<ID>) that enable an analysis of your use. In particular, the following are recorded: pages viewed and time spent on them, source of the visit (referrer), approximate location (country, region, city), device type, screen resolution, browser and operating system, and time of the visit. Google Analytics 4 does not store or log full IP addresses; the IP address is used only to determine the approximate location and is then discarded.

(3) We have configured Google Analytics to minimize data collection: Google Signals, advertising features and ad personalization are disabled. The cookies expire after no more than 14 months; the analytics data is deleted after 14 months. We have concluded a data processing agreement with Google.

(4) The information collected by Google may also be transferred to servers of Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

(5) The legal basis for storing and accessing information on your device is your consent pursuant to § 25(1) TDDDG; for the subsequent processing of your data, it is your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw your consent at any time via “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page; the Google Analytics cookies will then be deleted.

(6) For more information, please see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and the Google Analytics Terms of Service at marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/terms/de.

§ 8 Reference projects and external links

(1) The reference projects shown under “Projects” are hosted on our own server (www.webgipfel.com/referenzen/). This privacy policy applies to them; they do not embed any content from third-party providers.

(2) Our website contains links to websites of other providers. Data is only transferred to them when you click a link. The respective provider is responsible for the processing on those websites.

§ 9 Changes to this privacy policy

If the technology of our website or the legal situation changes, we will update this privacy policy. The version applicable is always the one bearing the date stated above.
