04 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does a website from webgipfel cost?

That depends on the scope – a single landing page takes less work than a website with many subpages.

In the initial consultation, we work out together what you really need. You then receive a clear quote, so you know exactly what to expect in advance.

How long does it take for my website to go live?

That also depends on the scope: a landing page can be completed much faster than a larger website.

We set the schedule together. Because you always speak directly with us, there are no long waits between the individual steps.

What is the difference between a website and a landing page?

A website presents your business comprehensively – with dedicated pages for services, team and contact.

A landing page focuses on a single goal, such as an enquiry, a booking or a purchase, and leads visitors there without distraction. The two often complement each other.

Do you also make sure I can be found on Google?

Yes. SEO is one of our three core services. Even while building, we pay attention to clean technology, fast load times and a clear structure.

We also optimize your content so that customers find you where they are searching – for new and existing websites alike.

Do you only work with businesses from Munich?

No. We are based in Munich and are happy to meet in person, but we work for clients throughout Germany and internationally.

Much can easily be coordinated online – and you still always speak directly with the people who build your website.
